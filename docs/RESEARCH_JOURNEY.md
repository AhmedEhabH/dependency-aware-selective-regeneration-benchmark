## Why negative findings are preserved

Every negative row above is preserved because it is a **tested empirical
boundary** that saves future researchers/engineers **time, API cost, and
effort** by ruling out an approach under this protocol:

  (it approves ~everything it sees) and it never saw reverse-1hop-only
  candidates, so an "AND verifier-approved" gate cannot rescue the FP tail

  remains the single frozen next instrument under a new strict verifier.

  ruled out DESCRIPTIVELY by `FULL_UNIVERSE_V2_CANCELLED_AS_NON_BINDING_ABLATION`

  rank-monotone signal would be a near-null rerun with forking-path risk.
- **Parent-only repository-history memory as a FINAL-SET rescue layer** — ruled
  out as the final policy by `PARENT_ONLY_REPOSITORY_MEMORY_RESCUE_V2_FAIL`

  (recovers 21.6%/27.1% of deep dense misses at the CANDIDATE level, vs 20.6%/

  still fails on djangoCMS (Delta-F1 CI crosses zero in A and B). Candidate
  generation alone cannot solve the final-set decision; the decision
  (threshold/rejection) layer remains the unsolved part on djangoCMS.

Each has an explicit **revisit trigger** (new hypothesis, new protocol, more
data, or a genuinely stronger signal) — never "re-run because we want a
different number".
